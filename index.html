How to Use This Tracker

Click to show instructions
  1. Fill in the Expense Name (e.g. “Groceries”).
  2. Enter the Amount you spent (e.g. 50.00).
  3. Choose a Category from the dropdown (Food, Transport, Rent, etc.).
  4. Click the Add Expense button.
  5. Your expense will appear in the Your Expenses table below.
